- `PII` should be redacted in screenshots.
- Anything morally objectionable, like graphic material or perhaps obscene comments and language should be redacted.

- `Add annotations` to the image like arrows or boxes to draw attention to the important items in the screenshot.
- `Add borders` around the images.
- `Crop the image` to only display the relevant information.
- `Address bar` showing the `url` should be included.

# Screenshots
> `When in doubt, use console output but if you must use a terminal screenshot, then make sure you are appropriately redacting information`.

- Use `terminal output` over screenshots of the `terminal`
- It's easier to redact, highlight. ie (command we ran in blue and part we want to call attention in red)
- looks neater in a document.
- Cut out unnecessary output and mark the removed portion with `<SNIP`
- Other than that, never alter the output.
- Make sure that all the `formatting is stripped `out before pasting to our document.
- **When redacting, make sure to `add black bars instead of blurring`, and make sure that the image is directly edited and the shape is not added on top of the image in word.
eg:
![[Pasted image 20260825171205.png]]


# Terminal
- Redact the `credentials, password hashes`
- for `password hashes` strip out the middle of them and leave the first and last 3 or 4 characters.
- For `clear text creds` , replace with `<Redacted>` or `<password redacted`.
- use `color-code` highlighting to show the `command that was run` and `interesting output`

# What Not to Arcihve
- if you gain access to a `network share with sensitive data`, it's probably best to `just screenshot the directory with the files in it `rather than opening individual files and screenshotting the file contents.
