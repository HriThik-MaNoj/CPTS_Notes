- There are five types of users on standard wp installation
	- **Administrator**: Adding deleting users and posts as well as editing source code
	- **Editor**: publish and manage posts including posts of other users
	- **Author**: publish and manage their own posts
	- **Contributor**: write and manage their own posts but cannot publish.
	- **Subscriber**: Standard users who can browse posts and edit their profiles.

##  Enumeration

##### Quick way to identify a WordPress site
```python
curl -s http://blog.inlanefreight.local | grep WordPress
```

##### Themes & Plugins
- Browse the site and page source and look for the theme in use, plugins installed and even the usernames if author names are published with posts.
```python
curl -s http://blog.inlanefreight.local/ | grep themes
```

```python
curl -s http://blog.inlanefreight.local/ | grep plugins
```

- After finding the themes and plugins, the next step would be to enumerate the versions.
- We can try navigating to the directories of each of the plugins to get the version number and then check for `CVE's` associated with them.
